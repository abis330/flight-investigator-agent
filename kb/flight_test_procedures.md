# Flight-test procedures (excerpt)

## Post-flight data review
Every sortie: confirm log QC score > 80, review phase timeline, takeoff and landing ground roll against the
STOL target (< 300 m at sea level, no wind), energy used, minimum battery voltage, any WARNING/ERROR messages
and deviations from the fleet envelope. Any fault must be classified by subsystem and severity and linked
to similar past incidents.

## Severity definitions
critical: loss of a primary control or propulsion function; ground the aircraft.
major: degraded function with workaround or reduced margin; fix before next flight.
minor: within tolerance but trending; monitor.

## Turns and maneuvers are not faults
Bank angles up to 25 deg and yaw rates up to 10 deg/s during waypoint turns are normal and should not be
reported as anomalies.
